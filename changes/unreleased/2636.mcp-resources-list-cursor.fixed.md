- **MCP `resources/list` paginates with opaque AEAD cursors when the
  application-master keyring is composed.** `Fts5SearchContentCatalogue` resumes
  from a sealed-capable scan position (500-pointer / 50-visible pages),
  `ContentResourceRegistry::listPage()` carries provider-local resumes, and
  `ContentResourceListCursorCodec` seals `nextCursor` under purpose
  `waaseyaa.mcp.content-resource-list-cursor.v1` with principal binding and
  application-enforced expiry. Without the keyring, cursors stay refused and
  `nextCursor` is omitted. Responses still expose no raw count or hidden
  position. Spec/README prose for #2636/#2220 updated accordingly.
