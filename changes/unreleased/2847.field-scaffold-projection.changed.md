- **Changed — `make:content-type` now projects fields from the registered authority (#2847, FW-FIELD-PROJECTION-01):** the manual scaffold no longer owns a field-type map. A field-package adapter derives admissible ids, value/property kinds, defaults and storage validity from the registered field plugins and rejects unknown or metadata-incomplete declarations. Manual and blueprint scalar emission now agree for `text` (`string = ''`) and `datetime` (`mixed = null`) without changing existing blueprint golden bytes. Authored `entity_reference:<target>` remains a deliberate manual shorthand carrying `target_entity_type_id`; blueprint relationships remain richer declarations that also own cardinality, requiredness, deletion behavior and relationship-registry output. Existing seeded publication and implicit registration-adoption refusal are unchanged. The actual command provider lazily injects the boot-scoped registry, including manifest field plugins. Direct callers of `MakeContentTypeHandler` and `ContentTypeScaffoldCompiler` must now explicitly supply `FieldScaffoldProjection`; the former implicit built-ins-only construction is removed. Remaining #2847 work includes richer field options and key prerequisites, command taxonomy/deprecations, complete generated schema/test intent, and packaged generate/boot/sync/create/read/update acceptance.
