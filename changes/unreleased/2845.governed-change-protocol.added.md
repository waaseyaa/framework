- **Added — ADR-025 names the governed-change protocol and its change-receipt envelope (#2845, ADR-025 D-14):** the artifact-plan decision previously fixed plan, preview, apply and recovery semantics for generation alone, leaving open whether schema migration (#1625/#2730/#2731) — which owns its own planner, executor and ledger — speaks the same lifecycle language. D-14 names those semantics as a domain-neutral contract of seven obligations (immutable digest-bound plan, side-effect-free preview, stale-plan detection, controlled apply, typed change receipt, verification, recovery) and fixes a closed receipt envelope (`receipt_id`, `protocol_version`, `authority`, `authority_version`, `operation`, `plan_digest`, `outcome`, `correlation_id`, `causation_receipt_id`, `decision_receipt_id`, `issued_at`, `domain_payload`) with an `applied`/`no_op`/`refused`/`failed`/`recovered` outcome vocabulary. A receipt begins at controlled apply or recovery: preview emits none, and pre-apply cancellation emits none either — cancellation is not a `no_op`, which means apply ran and found the end state already satisfied. Non-mutating terminal outcomes (`refused`, `failed`) still record. A recovery that precedes new work emits two causally chained receipts rather than collapsing two durable effects into one enum value. Correlation groups related work without implying a distributed transaction or cross-authority rollback, and a composite orchestrator may reference child outcomes but may never overwrite or restate them. "One durable ownership record" is fixed as one authoritative record *per lifecycle boundary*, so the existing audit, validation, migration and generated-state records stay separate where their ownership is explicit and non-overlapping. v1 **emits** receipts and retains none: obligation 5 requires the typed outcome, not its retention, so reconstruction across processes is conditional on a retention sink that v1 does not mandate. D-14.7 binds `ArtifactApplyResult` as the sole authorized binding, mapping its existing members under `domain_payload` and returning the receipt rather than persisting it: `.waaseyaa/generated.json` remains the ownership authority and the transaction journal the recovery authority, while a durable receipt sink is deferred to its own decision because its custody semantics — append failure, reconstruction limits, concurrency, fsync, retention, redaction and Windows append behaviour — are unsettled. D-14.6 separates the new **change** receipt from ADR-023's existing **decision** receipt and makes the link structural via the top-level `decision_receipt_id`, never by overloading `causation_receipt_id`. D-14.9 fixes `SiteInitializationService::CONTRACT_VERSION` as the sole machine-readable declaration of the generation authority's contract version, with monotonic compatibility semantics, and records that the adjacent `generator_version` already violates that rule. No schema binding, shared runtime code, or new package is authorized: D-14.8 requires a second real implementation before any abstraction is extracted, and states the conformance checklist that binding must satisfy.
