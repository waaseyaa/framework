- **Fixed — persistent queue messages without a handler were silently acknowledged (#2740):** `Worker` now raises a typed, payload-free `UnhandledQueueMessage` after exhausting its first-match handler roster. Unsupported messages follow the existing bounded retry/backoff policy and are recorded in the failed-job repository before rejection instead of disappearing. Successful void handlers, deliberate duplicate-occurrence acknowledgements, authority cleanup, and adapter-specific dispatch semantics are unchanged.
