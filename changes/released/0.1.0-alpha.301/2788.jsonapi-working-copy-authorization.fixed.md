- **Fixed — JSON:API `update()` authorizes the working revision it writes (#2788, FW-SITE-BLUEPRINT-01E independent review, critical):** `JsonApiController::update()` previously evaluated the entity-level `update` decision and every per-field `edit` decision against the `find()`-loaded published pointer, then loaded and saved the working copy (the tip revision) — so a disciplined revisionable entity whose published and working revisions diverged could be authorized from stale published inputs while a different revision was written. With value-dependent policies (the generated blueprint policies decide on the owner and `workflow_state` authorization inputs) that let a no-longer-owner keep editing, or blocked the legitimate owner of the tip. The controller now resolves the working copy first, checks entity-level update access and field-level edit access against that exact target, and only then hydrates the mutation precondition, applies attributes, and saves. Not-found and non-oracle behaviour, request validation order, revision-expectation semantics, denial status/body contracts and non-revisionable updates are unchanged. Regression: `JsonApiControllerWorkingCopyAuthorizationTest` drives the real controller over a repository whose working copy diverges from the published revision for both an ownership grant and a state-dependent field seal, in both directions, and proves nothing is saved before authorization.
